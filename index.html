Screenshot/screen-recording detection is best-effort only. Browsers cannot reliably detect screenshots. PrintScreen is a fairly confirmed signal (mainly Windows). Window-blur and tab-hidden are weak signals that can be triggered by ordinary app-switching. macOS Cmd+Shift+3/4/5, OBS, QuickTime, phone cameras, and any OS-level recorder are not detectable by a website — that is a hard platform limitation, not a bug.
